Forest School

Where the ancient cork oaks become classrooms and the rhythm of the seasons dictates the curriculum.

Deeply Rooted

"Our children don't just learn about the world; they learn within it."

The Homeschooling Collective

A supportive ecosystem for families dedicated to alternative education. We provide the physical space and the communal spirit for curated learning journeys.

From collaborative project-based learning to shared resources, the collective operates on a philosophy of trust, autonomy, and the belief that every child is a natural researcher.
