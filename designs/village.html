Our Ethos

Deeply Rooted in the Alentejo Earth

The Village isn't just a place to live; it's an ecosystem of shared intentions. Inspired by the traditional "montado" landscape, we've designed a space that breathes with the seasons.

Every structure is built with low-impact materials, respecting the natural topography of our sun-drenched valley. Here, your morning coffee is accompanied by the scent of wild lavender and the rustle of cork oaks.
