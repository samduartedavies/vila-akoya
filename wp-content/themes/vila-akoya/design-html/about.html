Our Story

Rooted in Passion, Built for the Future.

A tiny house village where creativity meets the earth, born from a dream to create a playground for our children and a sanctuary for the community.

"We didn't just build houses; we planted seeds for a shared life."

What is Vila Akoya?

Vila Akoya is more than a destination; it is a creative ecosystem. Conceived as a tiny house village, it represents a shift toward intentional living—where the architecture is as compact as the possibilities are vast.

It is a space designed for children to roam free and for adults to rediscover the art of shared existence. From the Campus to the Forest School, every corner reflects our commitment to a lifestyle that honors both individual creativity and collective harmony.

Where is Vila Akoya?

Nestled in the heart of Alentejo, we share our home with Camping Serro da Bica. This rugged, beautiful landscape provides the perfect backdrop for our vision.

A Vision of Shared Space

We believe that the future is collaborative. Vila Akoya was built for our children to grow up in a village—a real village where neighbors are friends and the forest is the classroom. We invite you to be part of this heart-led project, whether for a night at the campsite or a lifetime in the village.
