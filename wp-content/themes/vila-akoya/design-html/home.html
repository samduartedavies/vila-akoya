Spirit of Alentejo

Vila Akoya isn't just a destination; it's an experiment in living. We believe that when you strip away the noise of the modern world, what remains is essential: community, creativity, and the earth beneath our feet.

Our project was born from a dream of creating a self-sustaining ecosystem where artists, thinkers, and seekers can come together. From our Forest School to our communal campus, every inch of this land has been hand-tended with love and intention.
