A little village with a big heart.

That little village
we've been looking for

We wanted to live in the forest, and raise our kids amongst the trees. But we did not want to do it alone. Frankly, we're done with doing everything alone! Who came up with that? Lucky for us, there are loads more people like us. So we got a bunch of fantastic humans together and set out to build the little village of our dreams.

Expect to find something fantastic in every corner of this space. With playgrounds for children and for grown-ups, areas for wellness and relaxation, spaces designed for connection, creation and celebration, and a full agenda of workshops, classes and activities for humans of all ages.
