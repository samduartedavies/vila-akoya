Arete: The Homeschooling Collective & Activities Ages 5 to 13

Arete (noun): the pursuit of excellence through living fully in one's potential - of body, mind, and character.

We are a homeschooling family, and so are most of the families joining and visiting our project. Central to our kids' busy days is the homeschooling collective, where our kids study together with support of the parents and a homeschooling tutor.

Within the project we aim to create a place where our kids learn all about how to be a beautiful human in this world, where they make lifelong friendships and where we give them all the opportunities to find a passion, trade or art that will carry them through a life of inspiration and fulfilment.

The Coddiwomples
Ages 0 to 5

Coddiwomple (verb): To travel in a purposeful manner towards a vague destination.

For the littlest ones we also have loads to do! Join the Coddiwomples; with drop-in classes and activities -mostly outdoor- throughout the day. We also have on-site babysitters, available for private babysitting at most hours of the day.
