A Tiny Home Away From Home

One side of our project, which we call "The Village" is the more community-like part of our project; a car-free area with about 15 plots intended exclusively for tiny homes.

Here a diverse crowd of fantastic humans of all sorts of different backgrounds, cultures and ages, spend longer stretches of time with us in their own tiny homes and other kinds of unique accommodations.

We want this little village to be an inspiring place, so it is important to us is that these tiny homes are really beautiful, interesting, unique, weird, cute or absolutely fantastic, that they're built in a sustainable way and do not have a concrete foundation.

Every now and then we have some plots available for sale or for rent in our little village. If you'd like to be considered for one of these spots, please get in touch with us.
