A little home of your own
To stay as long as you like

The Village is the more community-like part of our project, and consists of a wonderfully diverse crowd of fantastic humans of all sorts of different backgrounds, cultures and ages.

Here we offer plots for people to join us for a longer term in their own tiny houses and all sorts of other unique living spaces. What is important to us is that it's built in a sustainable way, that it doesn't have a concrete foundation, and that it's beautiful, unique, weird or absolutely fantastic.

Every now and then we have some plots available for sale or for long-term-rental in our little village. If you'd like to be considered for one of these spots, please come and visit us and get to know us and this beautiful land. We would love to get to know you!
